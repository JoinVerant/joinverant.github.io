03 / SECURITY
Data protection

Your operating data is leverage. We guard it like it’s ours.

Production volumes, maintenance histories, cost structures — the information an audit touches is competitively sensitive. Protecting it isn’t a paragraph in our terms. It’s a design constraint on everything we run, including this website.

Least-data intake

We tell you up front what not to send. The audit needs task descriptions and hour counts — not well coordinates, not reserves data, not anything regulated.

No training on your data

We use AI providers under business terms that exclude your data from model training. Your operation’s numbers don’t become anyone else’s baseline.

Human eyes on every number

No figure reaches your report without a person tracing it back to its source. AI drafts; it never gets the last word on your money.

Encrypted in transit

Calls, files, and intake responses move over encrypted channels end to end. Nothing about your operation travels in the clear.

A site that carries nothing

This website sets zero cookies, runs zero trackers, and loads zero third-party scripts. It is locked down with a strict Content-Security-Policy — there is nothing here to steal because we never collect it.

You stay in control

Your materials are used for your audit and nothing else. Ask us to delete them when the engagement ends, and they’re gone.

07 / FAQ
Questions

What operators ask before starting.

How is this different from a generic AI consultant?
We don’t show up with slides and theory. We map your actual manual tasks to specific AI tools, attach conservative dollar and hour figures to each, and rank them by impact — so you leave with a prioritized plan you could act on tomorrow. And we work in oil and gas, not “every industry.”
How much does the audit cost?
$1,500–$2,500 depending on the size of your operation, quoted before you commit to anything — and credited 100% toward any build you commission, so if we end up building for you, the audit is effectively free. The discovery call costs nothing either way, and if we don’t think an audit is worth it for your operation, we’ll say so on that call.
Have you actually done this before?
We’re a new firm, and we won’t pretend otherwise — the sample audit is a worked example, clearly labeled, not a disguised client. What early clients get in exchange: both founders personally on the engagement, founding-client pricing, and a deliverable built so your own engineers can check every number against its source. That’s the deal — and it’s why the first question we answer on the call is whether we can actually help you, not how to close you.
Do we have to buy implementation?
No. The audit is the product. Plenty of operators take the report and run the changes with their own team. Implementation is there if you’d rather we build it — and only if the savings justify it.
Is our operational data safe?
Yes — and we can show you how, not just tell you. We’ll sign your NDA before the discovery call if you want one. We instruct you up front to keep sensitive or regulated data out of intake; we use AI providers whose business terms exclude your data from model training; a person reviews every AI output before it reaches you; and this website itself collects nothing — no cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts. The full approach is written down in our privacy & security policy.
Will this disrupt the field?
No. The discovery call and audit require nothing from your crews beyond a conversation. Any build we do is designed to fit your existing systems and roll out without pulling people off the job.
What does the audit actually look like?
One document: an executive summary with the conservative headline number, an opportunity matrix mapping each manual task to a specific AI fix with per-tool cost, and a phased 90-day roadmap. See a redacted sample here.

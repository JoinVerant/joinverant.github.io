03
Data protection

How we protect your data.

Production volumes, maintenance histories, and cost structures are competitively sensitive. We treat protecting them as a design constraint on everything we run, including this website.

Least-data intake

We tell you up front what not to send. The audit needs task descriptions and hour counts, not well coordinates, not reserves data, not anything regulated.

No training on your data

We use AI providers under business terms that exclude your data from model training. Your operation’s numbers do not become anyone else’s baseline.

Human eyes on every number

No figure reaches your report without a person tracing it back to its source. AI drafts, but a person has the final word on your money.

Encrypted in transit

Calls, files, and intake responses move over encrypted channels end to end. Nothing about your operation travels in the clear.

A site that carries nothing

This website sets zero cookies, runs zero trackers, and loads zero third-party scripts. It runs under a strict Content-Security-Policy. There is nothing here to steal because we never collect it.

You stay in control

Your materials are used for your audit and nothing else. Ask us to delete them when the engagement ends, and they’re gone.

Our name

Truth, put to work.

We built the name before we built the business, because we wanted the standard on the door.

VER
from the Latin “verus,” meaning true

The root of verify and veritas. It is the standard every number in our audits has to meet: traced to its source, checked by a person, conservative before optimistic.

+
ANT
from “grant,” to put into effect

To grant is to make something real: to commit, to deliver, to put into effect. A true number that never becomes a decision is just trivia.

When the two of us started Verant, we kept running into the same two failures in AI consulting: numbers nobody could defend, and reports nobody acted on. We built the fix into the name. Tell the operator the truth about where their hours and dollars are going, even when the truth is that AI will not help them here, then make that truth executable: ranked, priced, and ready to build. Every audit is held against both tests. If either one fails, it does not ship.

Questions

What operators ask before starting.

How is this different from a generic AI consultant?
We map your actual manual tasks to specific AI tools, attach conservative dollar and hour figures to each, and rank them by impact, so you leave with a prioritized plan you could act on tomorrow. We work only in oil and gas, not “every industry.”
How much does the audit cost?
Right now, nothing. The audit is free for founding clients. We are a new firm, and we would rather prove the quality of the work than charge for it before we have a track record. The paid part is implementation: if you want us to build what the audit recommends, we quote that up front before you commit to anything. If we do not think an audit is worth it for your operation, we will say so on the call.
Have you actually done this before?
We are a new firm, and we will not pretend otherwise. The sample audit is a worked example, clearly labeled, not a disguised client. The figures in it are placeholders until we publish our first case studies. In exchange, founding clients get a free audit while we build our track record, both founders personally on the engagement, and a deliverable built so your own engineers can check every number against its source. That is why the first question we answer on the call is whether we can actually help you, not how to close you.
Do we have to buy implementation?
No. The audit is the product. You are free to take the report and run the changes with your own team. Implementation is there if you would rather we build it, and only when the savings justify it.
Is our operational data safe?
Yes, and we can show you how rather than just tell you. We will sign your NDA before the discovery call if you want one. We ask you up front to keep sensitive or regulated data out of intake, we use AI providers whose business terms exclude your data from model training, and a person reviews every AI output before it reaches you. This website itself collects nothing: no cookies, no trackers, no third-party scripts. The full approach is written down in our privacy & security policy.
Will this disrupt the field?
No. The discovery call and audit require nothing from your crews beyond a conversation. Any build we do is designed to fit your existing systems and roll out without pulling people off the job.
What does the audit actually look like?
One document: an executive summary with the conservative headline number, an opportunity matrix mapping each manual task to a specific AI fix with per-tool cost, and a phased 90-day roadmap. See the worked example here.
